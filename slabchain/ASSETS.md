## Notes on individual slabs

- **#1 Eevee CHR (Pristine 10).** Largest single line in the inventory. Pristine 10 is CGC's highest tier and is rarer than CGC Gem Mint 10. Verify the cert # on `https://www.cgccards.com/certlookup/` returns matching photos.
- **#3 Magnemite 1st Ed Base.** Common card but the 1st Edition stamp from Base Set carries persistent collector demand independent of the Pokémon. Don't underestimate.
- **#7 Tyranitar AR (Pristine 10).** Ruler of the Black Flame is a very strong recent set; AR Tyranitar has strong appeal. Consider this anchor #2.
- **#11 Vivillon IR (Gem Mint 10).** Illustration Rares from Surging Sparks remain in print at time of writing — supply may still grow. Watch for population reports.
- **#12 Eevee 1st Ed Jungle.** This is **Jungle**, not Base. Jungle 1st Ed Eevee is a common with much lower value than people often assume — don't conflate with 1st Ed Base anything.
- **#13 Umbreon V (Brilliant Stars TG22).** Umbreon carries a persistent character premium. Of the alt arts in the inventory, this and #5 Mimikyu V are the long-term holds.

For the very first intake → mint → trade → redeem cycle (Phase 1), use the **lowest-value** cards to absorb operational mistakes cheaply. Suggested initial deposit:
